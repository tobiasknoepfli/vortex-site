Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Vortex is a desktop app for Windows made by Tobias Knöpfli ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data Vortex handles, where it goes, and what it is used for. In short: your data stays on your PC, Vortex has no servers and no accounts, and I never receive, see, sell or share your data.

1. Data stored on your PC

Everything you keep in Vortex (wheel items, notes, links, calendar events, clipboard history, settings and the like) is stored in a database on your PC, in your Windows user folder (%APPDATA%\Vortex). If you choose to keep the database in another folder (for example one synced by your own cloud drive, to use Vortex on several PCs), it is stored there, under that service's terms. The clipboard history and the Google sign-in are encrypted for your Windows account and never leave the PC they were made on.

2. Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. When you connect it, you sign in with Google in your browser and allow Vortex to access your calendars. Vortex then uses Google's Calendar API to:

  • list the calendars you may write to, so you can choose which one to sync with;
  • read the events of that calendar (title, times, place, description, repeats, reminders, colour);
  • add, change and delete events in that calendar when you add, change or delete them in Vortex;
  • read the email address of your Google account, only to show which account is connected.

This data travels only between your PC and Google, over an encrypted connection. It is stored in the Vortex database on your PC so the calendar works offline and the two can be kept in step. The sign-in (a refresh token) is stored on your PC, encrypted for your Windows account. Vortex does not send Google data to me or anyone else, does not use it for advertising, does not sell it, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

Disconnecting: Calendar › Settings › Disconnect stops the sync and revokes Vortex's access at Google. You can also remove Vortex's access at any time on your Google account's third-party connections page. Events already synced stay in both calendars until you delete them.

Vortex's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Other connections to the internet

Vortex only goes online for these features, and sends no personal data with them:

  • Calculator: current exchange rates (European Central Bank, currency-api via jsDelivr) and market prices (Yahoo Finance), fetched when you use them.
  • Downloads you ask for: FFmpeg from GitHub (for converting and editing media) and speech models from Hugging Face (for dictation). Dictation itself runs on your PC; your voice is not sent anywhere.
  • Links you open from Vortex open in your browser, under that website's own policy.

These services see your IP address when your PC contacts them, as with any website.

4. What Vortex does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking or crash reporting.
  • No accounts, and no servers of its own.
  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing of data.

5. Your control

You can change or delete anything in Vortex at any time. Uninstalling Vortex and deleting %APPDATA%\Vortex removes all its data from your PC. Since I hold no data about you, there is nothing for me to hand over or delete; if you have a question, write to me.

6. Children

Vortex is not directed at children under 16.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
