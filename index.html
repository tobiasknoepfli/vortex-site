A launcher wheel and everyday tools for Windows

Vortex is a desktop app for Windows by Tobias Knöpfli. A hotkey opens a radial wheel with your apps, folders, websites and tools, and Vortex brings the tools along.

What's in it

  • The wheel: apps, files, folders, websites and system actions on a ring, opened with a hotkey or the mouse.
  • Calendar with alarms, repeating events, imports, and an optional two-way sync with Google Calendar.
  • Notes, Links, Clipboard history, Calculator, Clock (timers and alarms), Capture (screenshots and recordings) and Dictation (speech to text, on the PC).
  • Convert pictures, videos and music, and Editors for audio (with lyrics), video and images.
  • Paths, Pixel, Transfer and window layouts for working with files, colours and screens.

Google Calendar

If you choose to connect it, Vortex keeps its calendar and one of your Google calendars in step, both ways: events added, changed or deleted on one side show up on the other. Vortex only uses this access for that sync. How it handles your data is described in the privacy policy.

Your data stays with you

Vortex has no accounts and no servers of its own. What you keep in it is stored on your PC, or in a folder you choose to share between your PCs. Vortex collects no usage data.
